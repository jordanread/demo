The Verdant Forge Collective

A fully realized fictional world — a speculative robotics homestead community presented as if it were real. Features a Three.js 3D hero, Leaflet farm map, Chart.js telemetry dashboard, and ~15,800 words of structured JSON content across 48 files. Built as an exercise in worldbuilding through code.
