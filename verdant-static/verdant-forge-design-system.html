⬡ Soil Systems · Experiment Log · Entry #047
Moisture Inference at the Edge:
What Month Four Actually Showed
Meridian Strauss 14 Oct 2025 12 min read
We reduced irrigation waste by 31% without losing a single yield point. But the model didn't learn what we expected it to learn.
Four months ago we deployed twelve soil probe nodes across the north and east fields, each running a lightweight regression model trained on three seasons of collective records. The theory was straightforward: push inference to the edge, reduce cloud dependency, and let the soil tell the bots what it needed before we had to ask.
Key finding: The model prioritized root zone temperature over surface moisture in 7 of 12 zones — directly contradicting our initial hypothesis.
The contradictions were instructive. Zone 3, which runs the tomato beds, developed a 4-hour predictive lead time on moisture demand...

Focus States (Accessibility)
Required: All interactive elements must have a visible focus ring. Use outline: 2px solid #4EBF5E; outline-offset: 3px; in dark mode and outline: 2px solid #3D6B4A; outline-offset: 3px; in light mode. Never use outline: none without a styled replacement. Keyboard navigation must be fully functional.

Touch Considerations

All tap targets minimum 44×44px. Hover states replaced by active states on touch. SVG map zones use touch events with tap-to-select behavior. Dashboard widgets show last 5 values on tap. No hover-dependent information — all tooltips accessible via tap.
