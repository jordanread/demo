Our approach

We're not trying to remove humans from farming.

We're trying to remove the parts that consume human attention without requiring human judgment — the routine carrying, the 3am system checks, the field measurements that someone has to take even when everything is fine.

What's left when those tasks are automated is farming work we think is better: observation, decision-making, tending, learning. We're testing whether that's true.
