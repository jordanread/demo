A Living Experiment

Verdant Forge began as a question: What happens if a small homestead community adopts the same automation technologies used by industrial agriculture — but applies them to regenerative, small-scale farming?

We built robots for harvesting, soil monitoring, irrigation, greenhouse tending, compost management, and livestock care. Now we share everything we learn.
