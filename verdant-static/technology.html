How we build

Build Philosophy

01

Right-sized compute

We run the smallest compute platform that can do the job reliably. Raspberry Pi Zero for logging, Jetson Orin for perception, ESP32 for sensing. Overprovisioned compute is wasted energy and heat.

02

Repair-first design

Every system can be diagnosed and repaired with tools in the Tool Barn. No parts that require proprietary calibration equipment or factory service. If Willem can't fix it on-site, we rethink the design.

03

Soil-safe operation

No hydraulic fluids. No synthetic lubricants that could contaminate soil. Electric drive systems only. Biodegradable or food-safe materials anywhere that contacts soil or crop.

04

Fail quiet

When a system fails, it stops and alerts a human — not continue in a degraded state. A stopped irrigation pump is recoverable. One that runs incorrectly for 6 hours is an expensive problem.

05

Open stack

Linux, ROS2, Python, open hardware. We don't use proprietary platforms where open alternatives exist. Vendor lock-in on a farm with a 20-year horizon is a genuine risk.
